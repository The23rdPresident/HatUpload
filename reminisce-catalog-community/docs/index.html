How to submit

  1. Find the original.

    Search by name or paste an item link. Roblox details fill automatically. Faces use a classic image; custom head shapes stay Heads.

  2. Make it yours.

    For a reskin, enter the new texture or decal ID. Set your suggested price and stock. Check Timed item if it should go off sale after a duration.

  3. Send it for review.

    The owner checks your submission and decides whether to approve it.
