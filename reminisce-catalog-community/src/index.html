How to submit

  1. Find the original.

    Search for a Roblox accessory, gear, face, head, or body package. Its asset ID and description fill in automatically.

  2. Make it yours.

    For a reskin, enter the new texture or decal ID. Set your suggested price, stock, and sale schedule.

  3. Send it for review.

    The owner checks your submission and decides whether to approve it.
