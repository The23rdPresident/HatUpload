Privacy notice

Effective October 9, 2026

This notice covers Reminisce Item Uploader, operated by the Reminisce uploader owner. Roblox is a separate service and handles Roblox sign-in.

Account verification

With your permission, Roblox supplies your Roblox user ID and username through its official sign-in service. When you enter a verification code in Reminisce, its game server supplies your registered Reminisce UID and username. These details confirm who is submitting an item and are visible only to you and the uploader owner. They are not added to item announcement webhooks.

We do not request or store your Roblox password, security cookie, or API key. Roblox access tokens are used briefly on the server to read your identity and are not retained. A secure, essential browser cookie keeps you signed in for up to seven days. Signing in again requires a new in-game confirmation.

Submissions and abuse prevention

The service stores the submitted item, its suggested settings, review outcome, and your verified identity. Your linked account can view its last 100 submissions and private decline reasons. Older submissions created before account verification use private receipts saved in the original browser. The service also uses hashed network addresses and short-lived rate counters to prevent spam. Cloudflare provides hosting, database storage, and Turnstile anti-abuse checks; Roblox provides sign-in and item information.

Contributor rewards

For verified submissions accepted and published, the service saves a private reward receipt and lifetime total. Recipient matching uses a keyed hash of the experience, Roblox user ID, and Reminisce UID; reward records contain no names or those IDs in plain text. The game stores the credited total with your profile and confirms delivery after saving its wallet credit. These records remain for delivery and prevention of duplicate credits, including after you remove your account link. Earned rewards stay available to the original account; unearned submissions lose eligibility when their verified identity is removed.

OAuth support pages

When GitHub Pages is configured for account linking, GitHub hosts the public sign-in information, policies, and callback page. GitHub logs visitor IP addresses for security. The callback receives a short-lived Roblox authorization code and state, removes them from browser history, and forwards them to the main uploader. It does not store these values in browser storage or exchange them for tokens. The uploader server checks the original browser cookie, one-time state, and PKCE proof before establishing a session. Roblox client secrets and access tokens stay on the uploader server.

Retention and removal

Verification codes expire after ten minutes. Expired sign-in attempts and browser sessions are cleared during daily maintenance. Expired code records are kept for no longer than one extra day before the next daily maintenance. Unused account links are removed after 90 days during daily maintenance. Declined item records are normally removed after 180 days; approved catalog and moderation records remain for operating the catalog.

To remove your account link, sign in and select Remove account link. This invalidates all your uploader sessions, removes the link, and removes your Roblox ID, Reminisce UID, and name from this service's submission records. The item details and review history remain. Items already published to the game are not withdrawn by removing your link. Existing browser receipts can be removed by clearing this website's browser storage. Contact the uploader owner through the Reminisce project's published contact channel for other data requests.

Roblox consent

You can also remove this application's authorization from your Roblox account settings. The uploader does not use Roblox authorization for background account actions. Use Sign out or Remove account link to end an existing uploader session immediately.
