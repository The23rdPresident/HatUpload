Terms of use

Effective October 9, 2026

Reminisce Item Uploader lets eligible users submit proposed items for the Reminisce game catalog. The service is operated by the Reminisce uploader owner and is not an official Roblox service.

Using the uploader

Use your own Roblox account and your own registered Reminisce account. Roblox determines eligibility for its OAuth sign-in. Do not share verification codes or enter someone else's code. Account verification is required for new public submissions. It is not required to play Reminisce. Linking an account by itself gives no reward.

Item submissions

Submit only items and textures you are allowed to use. Follow Roblox's applicable rules and respect creators' rights. Duplicate items and invalid settings can be declined. Verification establishes the submitter's identity; it does not establish ownership of an item or guarantee approval.

By submitting, you ask the owner to review the item and, if accepted, add it to the Reminisce catalog using its linked Roblox asset or texture. The owner may adjust settings, decline a submission, or stop publishing an item. Prices must be between 0 and 50,000 PnGs, limited stock between 10 and 500, and non-limited items have unlimited stock.

Contributor rewards

A verified community submission earns 100 in-game pNgs after the owner accepts it and publication succeeds. The game credits the verified account once per item; offline rewards wait until that account joins. Membership does not multiply this reward. Declined items, duplicates, unverified submissions, and owner-created entries earn nothing. pNgs are game currency, not Robux or money. Removing your account link preserves earned credits, but removes eligibility from submissions that have not earned a reward.

Service availability

Roblox, Cloudflare, and other dependencies can be unavailable. Publication and announcements can be delayed while the service retries. Do not spam requests or try to bypass account verification, rate limits, or owner access.

Your account

You can sign out or remove your linked account on the uploader. Read the Privacy notice for information about stored submission records. Contact the uploader owner through the Reminisce project's published contact channel for assistance.
